# Geopolitical and regional continuity

## Purpose and boundary

This method addresses concentrated exposure to countries, regions, currencies,
networks, cloud regions, suppliers, staff, customers, banks, custodians, and legal
entities. It connects changing sanctions, export controls, conflict, shutdowns,
currency restrictions, asset access, people safety, relocation, and exit to explicit
decisions and evidence.

It is not geopolitical prediction, legal advice, sanctions clearance, an evacuation
service, or permission for an agent to move people, data, hardware, credentials, or
funds. Applicable legal and trade decisions require current authoritative sources and
qualified jurisdiction-specific owners.

## Continuity loop

Use the eight questions in
[catalog/operations/geopolitical-continuity-questions.yaml](../catalog/operations/geopolitical-continuity-questions.yaml)
as one conditional loop:

1. map regional and correlated concentration;
2. identify current jurisdictional obligations and restricted flows;
3. test conflict, civil disruption, shutdown, border, banking, currency, seizure, and
   abrupt-regulation scenarios;
4. demonstrate legal, safe, secure, sufficiently capacious, and reversible alternatives;
5. protect and communicate with affected people;
6. identify assets that can become inaccessible or unusable;
7. connect authoritative early signals to staged decisions;
8. challenge every fallback for newly created harm and prohibited activity.

Record the analysis in the
[regional continuity register](../templates/regional-continuity-register.md).

## Exposure model

Do not measure regional concentration one category at a time. A provider, payment
rail, network route, corporate entity, critical employee group, data store, supplier,
customer segment, and currency may share the same underlying jurisdiction or failure
event.

For each exposure record:

- direct country, region, entity, currency, network, facility, cloud region, supplier,
  staff, customer, bank, custodian, licensor, and owner;
- control, beneficial ownership, hosting, processing, contracting, settlement, and
  enforcement jurisdiction;
- critical assets, data, permissions, people, and decisions involved;
- concentration and correlated dependencies;
- recovery time, substitution time, legal transfer constraints, and irreversibility;
- source, confidence, owner, and review trigger.

An unknown beneficial owner, controlling jurisdiction, data path, or asset custodian
remains a blocker when it can change legality or continuity.

## Current obligations

The obligation review can include sanctions and restricted parties, export and
re-export controls, controlled technology and technical assistance, import and customs
rules, ownership and investment restrictions, anti-boycott duties, data localization
and transfer rules, government-access obligations, licensing, currency and banking
controls, employment duties, and contractual restrictions.

Each conclusion records the precise subject, flow, parties, ownership, jurisdiction,
authority, version or effective date, qualified interpretation owner, conditions,
expiry, and next check. News, search, model memory, vendor summaries, and screening
tools may locate or triage evidence; they do not replace the underlying authority or
qualified decision.

When the applicable rule is disputed or changing, preserve competing interpretations,
limit exposure when consequence requires it, and escalate to the named authority. Do
not silently convert uncertainty into permission.

## Disruption scenarios

Test combined scenarios, including:

- armed conflict, civil disorder, emergency law, expropriation, or forced control;
- internet, telecom, power, cloud-region, satellite, or cross-border network loss;
- border closure, travel restriction, port closure, customs delay, or supply embargo;
- bank failure, payment disconnection, frozen accounts, currency controls, rapid
  devaluation, or inability to settle obligations;
- sudden prohibition of a service, technology, ownership structure, transfer, or
  customer relationship;
- loss of staff availability, safe communication, credentials, licenses, records,
  hardware, facilities, or enforceable contracts;
- disinformation, coercion, surveillance, retaliation, or targeting of staff, users,
  contractors, and partners.

Define duration, warning, affected parties, safe minimum operation, decision lead
time, inaccessible assets, and recovery conditions. Include a scenario in which the
preferred alternative fails for the same correlated reason.

## Alternative operation and relocation

An alternative is usable only when evidence supports:

- legal permission for the parties, technology, data, funds, contracts, and ownership;
- sufficient capacity, performance, security, support, and skilled staffing;
- safe data, credential, asset, and authority transfer;
- tested network, supplier, payment, and operational paths;
- preservation of privacy, labor rights, human rights, accessibility, and affected
  peoples' agency;
- clear entry, suspension, reversal, and exit conditions.

A second region in the same provider, legal entity, network corridor, banking system,
or controlling jurisdiction may not be independent. Paper diversification does not
count as continuity evidence.

## People protection

Human safety and agency take priority over uptime, asset recovery, public relations,
and forced availability. The plan defines local ownership, reliable and fallback
communication, privacy-preserving contact, emergency support, workload expectations,
leave and inability-to-work treatment, secure access reduction, evacuation or
relocation authority where appropriate, and protection from coercion or retaliation.

The framework cannot decide that a person should travel, remain, disclose a location,
or perform work during danger. Those actions require the affected person's agency,
qualified local judgment, and separately authorized support.

## Assets and access

Map contracts, accounts, funds, credit, licenses, data, backups, keys, credentials,
hardware, facilities, records, intellectual property, domains, registries, and legal
claims to their custodian and enforcement route. Test both technical recovery and
legal usability.

A copied asset may still be unusable because of law, license, ownership, key custody,
banking access, government control, or contractual enforceability. A legal right may
still be practically inaccessible. Record both dimensions.

## Signals and staged decisions

Use the external-sensing contract for official notices, regulator and court action,
restricted-party and ownership changes, border and transport status, bank and payment
availability, network reachability, supplier notices, staff safety reports, diplomatic
and security advisories, and qualified local evidence.

Predefine thresholds for:

- increased review or collection cadence;
- freezing new commitments or exposure;
- diversifying suppliers, assets, currencies, networks, or regions;
- limiting access, transfers, sales, or deployment;
- activating people-protection and communication plans;
- suspending operations;
- relocating or exiting.

Act on corroborated evidence and approved authority, not nationality, ethnicity,
rumor, collective suspicion, or a model's unsupported geopolitical prediction. Track
the maximum tolerable time from change to detection, interpretation, decision, and
protective action.

## Fallback challenge

Every fallback receives a new impact assessment. It may introduce unlawful transfers,
weaker labor or privacy protection, surveillance, censorship, corruption exposure,
environmental harm, discrimination, unsafe contractors, insecure infrastructure,
conflicted ownership, or dependence on coercive authorities.

Reject a fallback when the new risk violates a non-waivable obligation or exceeds
authorized residual-risk limits. Do not hide transferred harm under the label of
business continuity.

## Agent use and permissions

An agent can maintain the text register, retrieve authorized current sources, compare
versions, map affected graph nodes, prepare scenarios, and draft decisions. It must
record source boundaries, retrieval time, jurisdiction, uncertainty, and failures.

Separate explicit authorization is required before contacting people, subscribing to
services, changing customer or supplier relationships, moving data or credentials,
transferring funds, suspending operations, controlling infrastructure, or publishing
communications. A recommendation or generated runbook is not authorization to act.

## Gate conditions

A positive gate is blocked when:

- a material regional concentration or controlling jurisdiction is unknown;
- an applicable sanctions, export, ownership, data, banking, or other restriction has
  no current qualified interpretation;
- people protection is subordinated to continuity or lacks local ownership;
- an essential asset can become inaccessible without an approved contingency;
- the selected alternative is untested, unlawful, unsafe, correlated, or lacks
  sufficient capacity;
- thresholds and decision authority cannot act before options close;
- the fallback creates an unreviewed material legal, ethical, security, privacy,
  labor, environmental, or human-rights risk.

Completion means that each material exposure has an owned, current, exercised,
lawful, and safe response or an explicitly authorized residual risk with a review
trigger. It does not mean that geopolitical disruption can be predicted or prevented.
