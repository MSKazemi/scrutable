# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to help people ask better questions and disagree productively about
evidence. That only works if participation is safe and criticism is aimed at claims
rather than people.

**Expected**

- Critique the argument, the evidence, and the artifact — not the person.
- Say what would change your mind. Disagreement backed by a falsifier is welcome and
  valuable here; the framework's own method depends on it.
- Accept "unknown" and "I was wrong" as ordinary, respectable contributions.
- Assume good faith in ambiguity, and ask before escalating.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Personal, demographic, or identity-based attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Deliberately submitting fabricated evidence, invented citations, or falsified run
  results. In a project about assurance this is a conduct violation, not merely a
  quality problem.

## Scope

Applies in this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
reviews — and to any public space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report to **mohsen.seyedkazemi@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer only and
kept confidential. Include what happened, where, and any links; you do not need to have
a resolution in mind.

If your report concerns the maintainer, and you would prefer not to write to him
directly, say so in the message and it will be handled through the reporting channels
of whichever platform the conduct occurred on.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will respond as proportionately as the situation allows: a private
correction, a request to edit or withdraw a contribution, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban for severe or repeated violations. Enforcement decisions and their reasons
are explained to the person affected.

This project is maintained by one person. There is no separate conduct committee and no
appeal body — that limitation is stated plainly rather than implied by silence.
